## Question 1 — Hybrid-Cloud Security [25 marks]

An organization is transitioning to hybrid cloud, combining on-premises/private-cloud and public-cloud resources. Design a comprehensive plan that:

1. Identifies and explains relevant internal and external network-security threats. **(10 marks)**
2. Proposes mitigating technologies, processes, and policies. **(10 marks)**
3. Evaluates effectiveness, challenges/limitations, and ways to address them. **(5 marks)**

## Question 2 — Public Key Infrastructure [25 marks]

An organization will deploy PKI for secure communication among internal systems, external partners, and customers.

1. Analyze PKI components and how they secure communication, including CAs, digital certificates, and revocation. **(10 marks)**
2. Design a large-scale PKI deployment and detailed flow for key generation, certificate issuance, certificate management, and secure data exchange between internal/external parties. **(10 marks)**
3. Evaluate PKI limitations involving scalability, certificate management, and third-party interoperability, and propose mitigations. **(5 marks)**

## Question 3 — VPN and AAA [25 marks]

1. An SME with branches across Sri Lanka needs secure and reliable headquarters-to-branch connectivity.
   1. Recommend suitable VPN algorithms and connection technology for the requirements.
   2. State your analysis and VPN-technology recommendations. **(8 marks total)**
2. Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of point-to-point VPNs as the SME network grows in complexity and scale. **(8 marks)**
3. Evaluate AAA advantages/disadvantages and explain effective implementation for security and user experience. **(9 marks)**

## Question 4 — Cisco ASA, DMZ, and ASDM [25 marks]

1. Explain how to configure Cisco ASA for robust large-enterprise perimeter security, evaluating firewall rules, IPS, VPN tunnels, content filtering, ATP, high availability, monitoring, and logging. **(12 marks)**
2. Explain and evaluate effective DMZ implementation for a large university, protecting sensitive data while maintaining connectivity for students, faculty, and staff. **(8 marks)**
3. Discuss ASDM strategies and best practices for security, streamlined management, and optimized performance in large enterprise environments. **(5 marks)**
